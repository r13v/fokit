# Campaign builder

Seven campaign templates share one audience, channel, delivery, and scheduling
model, then diverge into genuinely different payloads. Templates that can
collect payment reuse a conditional payment section with fixed, flexible, and
recurring variants.

## Try the complete flow

Switch between the loaded edit draft and a new campaign. Change templates to
see inactive payload fields leave the transaction. Exercise all seven branches:
newsletter, product launch, event invitation, fundraiser, course release,
community update, and feedback pulse. Paid templates reveal amount and recurring
interval rules.

## What this form stresses

- Seven conditionally validated payload branches in one schema.
- Shared visibility helpers for audience, schedule, and payment behavior.
- Typed transaction additions cleaning every inactive variant.
- Query-backed audience segments and a keyed create/edit baseline.
- Channel-group validation and conditional date ranges.
- Separate create and update mutations selected outside editable state.
- A live preview derived from the same Form, Please snapshot submitted to the API.
